Small Group Questions

7:45 PM, 15 to 30 mins. Release groups to open gym once finished.

  1. Jesus says the whole reason he told them all of this was so their joy would be full. Does that surprise you? Why?
  2. "As the Father has loved me, so have I loved you." Already past tense. What does that change about the verse right after it?
  3. Jesus obeyed his Father, but the Father loved him before the world existed. So what was his obedience actually for?
  4. When you hear "keep my commandments," what's the first feeling that comes up?
  5. Where are you obeying God to earn something, instead of because you're already loved?
  6. Jesus' one command is to love each other. Who is hard for you to love right now?
  7. What's one way you can stay in his love this week?
